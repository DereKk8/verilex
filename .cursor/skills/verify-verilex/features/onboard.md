# Onboard

`verilex onboard <word>` admits a word that proves a claim, and is the only writer of `.verilex/grouping.yaml`. It needs two counted uses of the word in this product. It checks the claim's step-to-claim mapping and refuses evidence that shows only a declared non-proof. It matches the word's claim against the claims the vocabulary groups (same, match, ambiguous or new). Then trials replay the word's latest use on the healthy product and under every planted defect: the word must be green on the healthy product and red under its own claim's defects, and must behave like the words of a claim it matches. verilex calls no model: an ambiguous proposal whose word behaved like the other claim's words joins as a claim of its own and goes back to the calling agent as a decision request, which `--same-as <claim>` or `--distinct` answers. An onboarded word is admitted; its sealed decision is part of its stamp, and a hand edit or a new planted defect makes it drift-suspect.

## Sub-features

- `onboard-refused` refuses a word with counted uses in fewer than two runs of this product, and a word without a claim.
- `onboard-new` lets a claim that matches no grouped claim join the vocabulary: trials on the healthy product and under each planted defect, a sealed decision with `match: new`, and the word admitted.
- `onboard-stamp` makes the next run of a chain with the word live once (`word admission changed`), and onboarding an onboarded word again changes nothing.
- `onboard-same` groups a word that pins a grouped claim itself (`match: same`).
- `onboard-mechanical` groups a word whose claim matches a grouped claim mechanically, after it behaved like that claim's words on every trial state, with no decision request (`match: mechanical`).
- `onboard-ambiguous` onboards an ambiguous proposal's word with its claim as a claim of its own (`match: new`, the candidates under `pending:`), prints the decision request with exit `0`, refuses an answer the request does not offer, and groups the word under the claim the agent names with `--same-as` (`match: agent`).
- `onboard-gate` rejects a word that gives a false pass under its claim's planted defect, and leaves it provisional and the grouping file unchanged.
- `onboard-finding` rejects a word that behaves differently from the words of the claim its claim matches, and reports each disagreement as a finding with evidence.
- `onboard-non-proof` rejects a word whose evidence shows only a declared non-proof of its claim.
- `onboard-mapping` rejects a claim that maps a step its evidence contract does not prove.
- `onboard-sealed` keeps the grouping file unchanged through every other command, and makes a word whose decision was edited by hand drift-suspect until it is onboarded again.
- `onboard-defect-drift` makes every word gated against a claim, and every alias word grouped under it, drift-suspect when the claim declares a new planted defect.
- `onboard-product` makes an onboarded word useful for another product from two counted uses there, without trials.
- `onboard-cross-repo` refuses a claim source in another repository while its feature file has uncommitted changes, and records the repository and commit in the decision.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Use the word in at least two chains of this product, then run `verilex onboard <word> [--json]`.
- When onboarding prints `undecided` and a `decide:` question, read both claims and answer with one of the two commands it prints: `verilex onboard <word> --same-as <claim>` or `verilex onboard <word> --distinct`.

## Gotchas

- Onboarding counts only live uses that proved the claim version the word pins. Once a word is admitted, a chain that already ran green skips, so use a second chain (another item name) for a second use.
- A word copied from `item-stored` keeps its `inputs` and `env`, so its trials see `TALLY_DEFECT`. Planted defects reach every frame step and word of a trial through the environment.
- Trials run outside the run history: `verilex runs` lists no onboarding trial, and the ledger never learns from one.
- `onboard` exits `1` only for `rejected`. `undecided` exits `0`: the word is admitted under its own claim until the agent answers. `inconclusive` exits `2` like a refusal, but prints the outcome on stdout and keeps a record.
- An answer (`--same-as`, `--distinct`) runs no trial and writes no record; it seals the decision onboarding already gated.
- The behavioral check compares at most three admitted words of a candidate claim, in word order.
- A `sed` that rewrites `$K/item-added.yaml` changes `item-added`'s sources too, so pin the prose `verilex claims` names after mapping another sub-feature.
- Restore every word, claim and feature file the steps added before the next recipe, or relaunch.
